Privacy Policy

This page describes how the desk. app (a personal study & work tracker) handles data.

What this app is

desk. is a personal-use tool. It is not a public product and is not used by anyone other than its owner.

What data it stores

All notes, tasks, timetable entries, and other content you create in the app are stored directly in your own browser (localStorage) and, optionally, in a private file inside your own Google Drive account's app-data space — a storage area that is not visible in your regular Drive file list and is only accessible by this app.

Google account access

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app requests permission to read and write a single private backup file in your Drive's app-data space. It does not access, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive, and it does not share your data with any third party.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

This app does not use cookies, tracking scripts, analytics, or advertising of any kind.

Contact

For any questions about this app or its data handling, contact the owner directly.
